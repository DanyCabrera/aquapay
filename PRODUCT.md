# AquaPay

register: product

## Users

Tesoreros and one or two administrators of the community water service in Aldea Sibaná, El Asintal, Retalhuleu. They work at a desk in the community hall, often with a paper receipt book still nearby. They type DPIs, issue numbered receipts, and look up whether a household already paid the year. They are not software people. They need the next field, not a tour.

## Product purpose

Operate the annual water-fee ledger: register households, record payments (annual fee or nozzle purchase), print a sequential receipt, and consult history. Design serves the task. It should disappear into issuing receipt 047.

## Brand personality

Municipal clerk, not a startup. Quiet, specific, slightly warm like receipt paper. Spanish that a neighbor would say at the ventanilla. No hype, no "plataforma", no "bienvenido de nuevo".

## Voice

- Short labels. Verbs that match the job: Emitir, Consultar, Registrar.
- Place names stay: Aldea Sibaná, El Asintal, Retalhuleu.
- Money as `Q 120.00`. Receipts as `047`. DPI as 13 digits.
- Errors say what to do next.

## Anti-references

- Water-startup teal, waves, droplet logos
- Vercel / Linear black-and-white dashboards
- SaaS hero metrics: four identical icon cards and a greeting
- Inter + rounded-2xl + soft shadow on every panel
- Purple gradients, glassmorphism, grid backgrounds
- "Boost productivity", onboarding cheer, fake activity feeds

## Design principles

1. A receipt counter is the hero, not a KPI collage.
2. Tables and forms beat cards. Cards only when grouping is the job.
3. One accent, used for the primary action. Neutrals do the rest.
4. Familiar office software, not a brand experience.
5. If it looks generated, rewrite the structure, not the color.
